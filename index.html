Before your first visit

Yes — most oral antihistamines suppress skin test reactions, so we ask patients to pause them for 5–7 days before a skin-prick appointment. Asthma inhalers, nasal steroids and most other medications can be continued. We send a personalized prep list when you book.

Diagnostic allergy testing is covered by most major plans. Our team verifies your benefits and provides a written estimate before your appointment, so there are no surprises on the day.

Most patients notice meaningful improvement within 6–12 months of starting build-up, with full benefit after 3–5 years of maintenance. Progress is tracked at every visit and visible in your patient dashboard.
