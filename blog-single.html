Shots vs. Drops: Understanding Immunotherapy

MC
Dr. Michael Chen
• Sep 28, 2025

Immunotherapy is the only disease-modifying treatment for allergies. Rather than simply suppressing symptoms with antihistamines, it fundamentally changes how your immune system responds to allergens.

Subcutaneous Immunotherapy (Allergy Shots)

Allergy shots are the most proven and traditional method. They involve regular injections of a highly personalized serum containing tiny amounts of your specific allergens. Over time, the dose is increased, building your tolerance.

Sublingual Immunotherapy (Allergy Drops/Tablets)

Sublingual immunotherapy (SLIT) involves placing drops or a tablet under the tongue daily. This is often preferred by pediatric patients and those who cannot commit to weekly clinic visits during the build-up phase.
