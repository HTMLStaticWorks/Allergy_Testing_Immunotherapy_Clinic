Diagnostics

Allergy Skin & Blood Testing

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective treatment. We utilize both rapid skin prick testing and specific IgE blood tests to identify exact environmental, seasonal, and pet allergens.

  • Process: Minimal discomfort, utilizing sterile disposable applicators.
  • Duration: Results are typically readable within 15-20 minutes for skin tests.
  • Detection: Pollen, mold, dust mites, animal dander, and insect venom.
Note: Most insurance plans cover diagnostic allergy testing. We will verify your benefits prior to your appointment.

Treatment

Immunotherapy

Rather than just managing symptoms, immunotherapy retrains your immune system to tolerate allergens. This provides long-term, structural relief from severe allergies and asthma.

  • Subcutaneous (Shots): The gold standard. Administered on a structured schedule (build-up and maintenance phases).
  • Sublingual (Drops/Tablets): A convenient at-home alternative for specific allergens.
  • Tracking: easily monitor your shot schedule via the AllerPure Patient Dashboard.

Ongoing care

Food Allergy Management

Food allergies require precise diagnosis and careful management. We provide comprehensive care for both children and adults dealing with food sensitivities and severe allergic reactions.

  • Diagnostic Oral Challenges: Safely conducted in-clinic under direct medical supervision.
  • Dietary Guidance: Structured elimination diets and nutritional counseling.
  • Action Plans: Downloadable, personalized anaphylaxis emergency plans available directly in your portal.
